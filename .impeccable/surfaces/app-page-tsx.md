# Surface brief: landing page (/)

Scope: the single-scroll landing page. Mode: Persuade. Action: open a WhatsApp chat (wa.me), every CTA one label, "Chamar no WhatsApp". Proof: real photos 01-05, Google 5,0 with 84 reviews, 5 years, 2.000+ services, flyer service list. Constraint: strictly brand colors (#131351 navy, #FCA700 orange, #77B900 green, #FFFFFF); puzzle-piece imagery pinned by the client; "Tirar ruído" switch turns the whole page white, flat, static.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The salon as a puzzle that fits: every block of the page is a jigsaw piece cut to lock into the next, refusing the category default of a photo hero over a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated flat fields of navy, orange and green owning whole sections, joined by jigsaw-tab seams; photos clipped into real interlocking puzzle pieces; Barlow Condensed ExtraBold display echoing the EDIVERTIDO wordmark, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body; pill buttons, navy text on orange and green, white on navy.

STORY: A full salon for everyone in Recife (cut, beard, brows, kids, styling); the reason it is different is that it was built for autistic and neurodivergent people; real photos prove hard cuts still get finished; the visitor taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. Left 6 columns: headline in white condensed display, 2 lines, subtext under 20 words, orange "Chamar no WhatsApp" pill. Right: four jigsaw pieces (three real photos plus one orange piece carrying the lemniscata) that fly in and snap together into one square on load and can be nudged by drag. Header carries logo, the "Tirar ruído" switch and the WhatsApp pill. Bottom edge is a jigsaw seam into the orange services band.

FORM: Client-pinned puzzle world (neurodiversity community vernacular), fused with the roll's discipline of one committed color per section. Seed key 62b346e8 (roll overridden by client pin).

SIGNATURE: the hero puzzle assembling; reprised once as the "Como funciona" chain of six pieces locking in sequence on scroll. In "Tirar ruído" mode both become still, white, flat.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
